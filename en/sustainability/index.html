Sustainability

Sustainable trade is durable trade.

We believe international trade must take responsibility. Not as a marketing feature, but as a decisive filter in every decision we make.

Sustainability

Responsibility across the full value chain.

We assess partners not only on price and quality, but also on ecological and social standards. Sustainable trade is durable trade.

Clean Energy

Priority for renewable energy sources and low-emission transport chains.

Social Responsibility

Fair working conditions and ESG-vetted partners across the whole chain.

Local Impact

Long-term partnerships with regional producers rather than short-term deals.

Our commitments

  • Annual ESG audits of our key suppliers
  • Priority on low-carbon transport routes (rail and sea before air)
  • Transparent documentation of countries of origin
  • Partnerships with local organisations in sourcing regions
  • No cooperation with suppliers under suspicion of child or forced labour

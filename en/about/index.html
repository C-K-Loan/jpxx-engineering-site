About us

Industrial trade requires trust, process and global reach.

JPXX Engineering GmbH is an owner-managed German trading company specialising in technical products, industrial plants and components. We bridge manufacturers, suppliers and end customers across three continents and handle the full process end to end.

Our values

  • Reliability: Agreed timelines are met. Our partners know what to expect.
  • Transparency: Open communication on costs, risks and schedules. No hidden markups.
  • Technical depth: Technical products require technical understanding. Our consultants know your industry.
  • Sustainability: We audit partners against ESG criteria and favour efficient transport routes.
